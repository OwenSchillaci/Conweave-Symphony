SYMPHONY FIGURE 4 RECONSTRUCTION

Reference: https://arxiv.org/html/2604.16880v1#S4.SS2
Published comparison values: across 50 runs, baseline maximum step overlap
24–35 and Symphony maximum step overlap 3–6. These are reference values, not
acceptance thresholds. Exact Figure 4 channel placement and propagation
delay were not published in the paper.

Assumptions and settings
32 hosts, four ToRs and four spines in a single spine group; no core tier;
three links per inter-ToR path; 10 Gb/s and 1 us per link. Four distinct
32-rank channel orderings share the 32 physical NICs and one Symphony
collective ID. Each of 7,936 channel transfers sends 8,000,000 payload bytes,
for 63,488,000,000 bytes (about 63.5 GB) per full-size collective, before
headers, ACKs or retransmissions. Channel count, shared NICs and per-transfer
payload are reconstruction assumptions. No injected host delay or background
traffic. RDMA uses the checkout's 1,000-byte packet payload, ACK interval 1,
and existing DCQCN/PFC defaults. IRN is disabled. ECMP uses a seeded five-tuple
hash and fixed flow tuples across treatments.

RED unit check: SwitchMmu::ConfigEcn multiplies KMIN_MAP and KMAX_MAP by 1000.
This setup uses 50,000 and 100,000 bytes of MMU shared egress-queue occupancy
and Pmax=0.2. These match the paper's printed numeric settings. The paper's
Figure 4 setup does not specify threshold units beyond those settings.
The packet traces report the device egress queue's total queued bytes as a
separate congestion observation. Symphony uses adaptive alpha, k=0.01,
100 us windows, a 25% outpacing threshold, and the checkout's 32-packet
sample guard. Late start tracks progress before activation and starts with
alpha 1 and fresh counters at 500 ms.

Measurement
Collective-wide overlap counts a logical step once whenever any rank in
any of its four channels has a transfer in flight, from QP start to final ACK.
Same-egress overlap counts distinct logical steps observed departing the
same port within a 100 us bin. It is a second metric with a different
scope, and not the number of in-flight steps on that link. The analytical
lockstep line ends at the 1.5872 s per-NIC payload serialization floor.
The ECDF uses one sample per full-size completed collective and only
includes seeds with both an off and an on result. The smoke run is excluded.

Runtime and outputs
Seed-1 full-size results are under bytes-8000000/seed-1. Each run records
wall time, peak resident memory, raw and compressed log size, and its exact
simulator command. Detailed decisions and RDMA feedback are capped at
10,000 records per switch or feedback type; switch mark totals are complete.

Seed 1 trace checks
In the full-size baseline, switch 35 port 9 transmitted step-0 flow 4526
(channel 2) at 38,600,199 ns and step-1 flow 6697 (channel 3) at
38,607,976 ns. Both were in flight in the same 100 us bin, where the
measured device egress backlog reached 74,408 bytes. Flow 4526 completed
at 79,383,291 ns, and its same-channel dependent step-1 flow 4589 was
released at exactly that timestamp. This shows shared egress use and a
real ACK-gated dependency in the unmodified baseline scheduler.
Late start: all four channels were active at simulation time 510,000,000 ns
(500 ms after collective launch). The 834 transfer completions before
activation matched the baseline. The earliest Symphony mark was at
510,003,270 ns, 3.270 us after activation. There were no Symphony marks
before activation.
For those same flow IDs in the always-on run, flow 4526 completed and
released flow 4589 at 74,195,366 ns, 5,187,925 ns earlier than baseline.
The competing step-1 flow 6697 started at the same time in both runs
and completed 42,674,665 ns later with Symphony. This trace demonstrates
the dependency benefit and the bandwidth-shift cost for this specific
pair; the aggregate CCT determines the net effect.

Dedicated NIC variant
Use --dedicated-rails with analysis/run_symphony_figure4.py to select
the four-NIC-per-host topology. The four logical rings use one NIC each
in host-link file order and share the existing leaf-spine fabric.
Output goes under dedicated-rails/ in the requested output directory.
Each summary includes transmitted bytes and utilization per directional
port over the collective interval. On dedicated access links it also
includes data and feedback counts and checks that packets use the port
for their rail endpoint IP. The ideal payload floor for the 8 MB
dedicated case is 0.3968 s at 10 Gb/s per NIC.
